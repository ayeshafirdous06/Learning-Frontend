The Edge Of Seventeen

The Edge of Seventeen (2016) is a coming-of-age comedy-drama about Nadine, an awkward high school student whose life becomes chaotic when her best friend starts dating her older brother. As she deals with friendship, family, and first love, she learns important lessons about growing up and accepting herself.
