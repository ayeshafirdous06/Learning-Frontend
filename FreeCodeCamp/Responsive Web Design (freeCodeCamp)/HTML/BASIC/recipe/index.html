Honey Garlic Chicken

This Honey Garlic Chicken is a sweet, savory, and sticky dish that takes less than 15 minutes to make

Ingredients

  • 2 deboned chicken thighs (skin on)
  • 2 big spoons of soy sauce
  • 2 big spoons of honey
  • A little bit of oil (from the chicken)

Instructions

  1. Sear: Cook chicken over medium heat for 3 minutes per side.
  2. Dice: Remove chicken from pan and cut into bite-sized cubes.
  3. Combine: Return chicken to pan. Pour in soy sauce and honey.
  4. Thicken: Simmer on medium-high heat until sauce turns into thick syrup.
  5. Serve: Remove from heat and serve immediately over hot rice.
